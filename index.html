About

Okay, fine, here's my story

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at the University of Ghana who loves turning ideas into websites people actually enjoy using. I work mainly with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and I'm steadily growing in Python and Java.

I've worked in a team to build a real restaurant ordering system, where I led DevOps: repository setup, CI/CD pipelines and architecture documentation. I care about code that's readable, responsive on any screen, and ready to ship.

Built to Ship
